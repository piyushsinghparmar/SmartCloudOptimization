# Suggested references

Use your course textbook and cite the sources you actually consult. These are good starting points:

1. Hillier, F. S., & Lieberman, G. J. *Introduction to Operations Research*. McGraw-Hill.
2. Taha, H. A. *Operations Research: An Introduction*. Pearson.
3. Osborne, M. J. *An Introduction to Game Theory*. Oxford University Press.
4. MATLAB documentation, `linprog` — https://www.mathworks.com/help/optim/ug/linprog.html
5. MATLAB documentation, optimization problem formulation — https://www.mathworks.com/help/optim/ug/linear-programming.html

Access dates and edition details should be added according to the versions you use.
